What this is

Travis County's two criminal trial courts, as reported by the Texas Office of Court Administration (OCA). District Courts handle felonies and serious misdemeanors; County Courts at Law handle Class A/B misdemeanors such as DWI, assault, and theft.

This is not Austin Municipal Court, which handles Class C offenses like traffic, parking, and noise. The source reports case volumes only — no charges, sentences, or disposition details.

How to read the charts

Filed is a flow: new cases entering the system. Resolved is a flow: cases leaving it. Pending is a stock: everything still open at that moment, so it moves slowly and never resets to zero.

Trends only turn in your favor when resolved keeps pace with filed. When filed runs above resolved for months, the pending line climbs — that is the backlog growing.

Why the newest months are missing

OCA refreshes this dataset monthly, but every refresh stops short of today.
